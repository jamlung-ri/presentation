Background · pre-meeting

What happened

Reported via Slack by the USDA/Gravity team ahead of a Monday meeting with USDA experts; resolved over that same weekend.

ItemDetail
Issue A new answer list requested for the USDA 6-item food security instrument was applied to all three related instruments (6/10/18-item), because the team reused the existing answer-list code LL5588-0 and changed its content rather than creating a new list for the 6-item. This also renamed answer LA30983-3 from "High food security" to "High or marginal food security," affecting instruments already implemented downstream.
Fix delivered LL5588-0 reverted to its LOINC 2.82 content, re-bound under the 10-item and 18-item panels. A new answer list LL7622-5 was created specifically for the 6-item, carrying the condensed wording USDA had asked for. Scoring was deliberately left off both (it varies by instrument), pending incoming USDA/CHW scoring guidance expected to land with a future release.
Reception USDA/Gravity team confirmed the revert addressed the critical need, and called the new condensed 6-item answer list an unexpected bonus.
Status as of Aug 24 Fix applied in working data; whether it's visible in an official LOINC release yet was still to be confirmed — 2.83 had already shipped when this was found.

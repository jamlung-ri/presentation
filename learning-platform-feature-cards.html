Card 1 of 8
Paid Certification

Courses are free to take. Learners pay a fee to earn a verified credential from the organization.

Seen in
OHIE Academy · $22 HL7 · multiple tracks SNOMED CT · certification exam Coursera · verified certs IU Expand · CE marketplace
✓ Worked well when
  • The org's credential carries real weight with employers
  • Tied to a professional or compliance requirement
  • Low-cost cert + high volume of learners
✗ Struggled when
  • Cert not recognized outside the org's own community
  • Audience can't afford even a small fee (LMIC context)
  • No capacity to maintain renewal or exam integrity
Values tension: Keeps content free while creating a revenue layer. Works with our open-access values — but only if we can waive fees for learners who can't pay.

Card 2 of 8
Educator Certification

The organization certifies who is qualified to teach the content, not just those who completed it.

Seen in
HL7 · certified trainer program Red Cross · first aid instructor cert Medical CME providers
✓ Worked well when
  • Org needed to scale delivery without scaling its own staff
  • Quality of teaching was mission-critical (clinical, safety)
  • Created a community of invested advocates
✗ Struggled when
  • Content evolved faster than re-certification cycles
  • Enforcement was difficult across a distributed network
  • Added complexity before core catalog was established
Values tension: Protects quality and the Regenstrief brand, but adds a gatekeeping layer. Important if IU faculty or external partners contribute content.

Card 3 of 8
Cohort-Based Learning

Learners move through content as a group over a set time period, with peer interaction, live sessions, or coaching built in.

Seen in
HL7 · FHIR training cohorts Virginia's housing training program HIMSS · bootcamps Maven Clinic · professional dev
✓ Worked well when
  • Learners needed to apply content, not just consume it
  • Peer discussion and accountability drove completion
  • Org wanted to build a professional community, not just teach
✗ Struggled when
  • Hard to coordinate across time zones (LMIC learners)
  • Required facilitator capacity the org didn't have
  • Drop-out rates hurt completion metrics
Values tension: High-impact but high-cost to deliver. May favor learners with schedule flexibility — which tends to exclude the LMIC workforce we most want to reach.

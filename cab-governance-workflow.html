LOINC Change Requests

Governance and workflow

How a proposed change becomes an approved, traceable decision and moves into implementation.

Decisions repository onboarding1 / 15
Nothing enters this repository until it is ready to be canon.

The decisions repo is for durable rules, policies, production changes, historical backfills, and traceable handoffs. Exploratory work belongs elsewhere until there is a specific change to approve.

The operating rule2 / 15
What belongs here

A Change Request is a commitment point.

Use a CR

When the team is ready to approve a durable change

  • A modeling convention, validation rule, policy, or production workflow change
  • A project outcome moving into implementation
  • A prior decision that needs to be made searchable and traceable
  • Downstream documentation, validation, release, or communication work needs a decision anchor
Use another space

When the work is still being shaped

  • The team is still comparing options
  • The work needs iterative design before there is a clear decision
  • The conversation is mainly task management
  • The issue would capture uncertainty instead of an approvable change

Forum levels

Review happens at the level affected by the change.

Internal leadership + advisors

Default — all changes route here

Modeling, policy, and governance decisions. Advisors are included as direct CAB reviewers. Sign-off is group consensus, not a single approver.

Additional input paths — managed by Tech Team off-thread
LOINC sub-teams

Cross-team impact

Release processes, tooling, or workflows used by multiple teams. Sub-team routing will be refined as the process matures.

Committee

Domain input

Lab, survey, document ontology, or external partner standards. Tech Team collects input off-thread; committees do not review GitHub issues directly.

Community

Post-approval validation

Used after internal approval for Large or Unknown scope changes. Tech Team posts to the LOINC Forum; community cannot reject a change.

Forums are cumulative when needed4 / 15
Triage — future routing

As the process matures, routing will follow the impact of the change.

For now: All changes route to Internal Leadership + Advisors, regardless of content area. The table below describes where routing is heading once the process is established.

Scope sizing

Scope describes how broadly the change reaches, not how hard it is.

SizeCriteria
TargetedAffects a bounded, enumerable set of terms or a single workflow step. No new rule or policy established.
ModerateAffects a named category of terms, a team workflow, or establishes or changes a rule. Impact is predictable but non-trivial.
LargeAffects a major section of LOINC content, cross-team workflows, public guidance, or a heuristic used broadly across many LOINCs.
UnknownScope cannot be reliably estimated before triage or implementation. Treat as Large for routing purposes.
Scope informs community review routing, not approval requirements6 / 15
End-to-end flow

Every CR moves through six practical stages.

Step 1Open and triageAssign type, scope, urgency, labels, and reviewer path.
Step 2CAB reviewApply cab-pending, post the review comment, and tag reviewers.
Step 3Decision recordDraft YYYY-MM-DEC-NNN.md, review via PR, merge to main, and update the index.
Step 4RecommendationsFinalize downstream recommendations in the CR issue.
Step 5ImplementationCreate child issues or record external tracking.
Step 6ClosureClose once all work is resolved or linked.

CAB outcomes

The review window creates one of three outcomes.

Approved

Move toward canon

Apply cab-approved. If community review is not needed, proceed to decision record and downstream planning.

Rejected

Close with rationale

Close the CR issue and preserve the reason so future proposals can understand the decision context.

Needs discussion

Return to active review

Apply back-for-discussion, extend or reopen the review window, re-ping reviewers, and escalate to a meeting if consensus still does not form.

Back-for-discussion is active, not deferred8 / 15
Community review

Community feedback informs the decision; it does not replace approval.

Use this optional step after internal approval when Large or Unknown scope changes may have uncertain user-facing impact.

RequestReviewer adds "request community review" to the approval comment.
PostTech Team applies community-review and posts a plain-language summary to the LOINC Community Forum.
WaitAllow a two-week response window.
ResolveNo substantive objections proceed to implementation; concerns return to back-for-discussion.
Authority remains with internal leadership9 / 15
Decision categories

The category tells reviewers what kind of artifact they are approving.

Modeling convention

Normative rules for how LOINC terms should be modeled.

Validation rule

Machine-checkable rules tied to a modeling convention or rationale.

Operational rule

Recurring team practices, ownership rules, or workflow changes.

Release policy

Release numbering, cadence, publication rules, or monthly release process changes.

Public guidance

Changes to Users' Guide, Community Forum, FAQ, release notes, or other user-facing guidance.

Historical backfill

Prior decisions reconstructed from transcripts, meeting notes, or Basecamp threads.

Roles

Each group has a distinct job in the workflow.

1

Requester

Opens a clear CR with enough context to make the proposed change approvable.

2

Internal leadership + advisors

Primary CAB reviewers. Approve, reject, request more discussion, or request community review when user impact is uncertain. Almost all changes route here.

3

Tech Team

Manages labels, review comments, decision records, task spawning, external tracking, and closure hygiene.

Governance succeeds when handoffs are explicit12 / 15
Example path

Survey parent questions and OrdObs tests the full workflow.

Triage

Modeling convention plus validation rule

Route to internal leadership and relevant survey or clinical domain expertise.

Review

CAB resolves the rule

Reviewers approve, request more discussion, or ask for community review if impact is uncertain.

Record

Create the decision file

Commit the canonical decision and update the index so the rule is discoverable.

Implement

Spawn downstream tasks

Update modeling conventions, validation rules, Users' Guide text, examples, and release notes if visible.
